The idea

Koshary is science. We just wrote down the formula.

Every bowl of koshary is a balance: soft rice, firm pasta, earthy lentils, nutty chickpeas, tangy tomato sauce and the crunch of fried onions. Get one layer wrong and the whole bowl feels off.

The Koshary Theory started with a simple question: what makes the perfect bowl? We tested ratios, fried onions in a hundred ways and tuned our sauce until it was just right.
